# Wally portfolio

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and prospective teammates reviewing Wally Mostafa's
resume and selected work. They need to identify relevant experience, see concrete
project evidence, and reach a case study, matched resume, or contact link quickly.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio spanning account leadership, UX/product strategy, and practical
AI implementation. The homepage presents a broad preview; JD routes select and order
the relevant evidence and remain supporting material for their matched resumes.

## Brand Personality

Warm, direct, and hands-on. Wally's name, voice, established typography, clay accents,
and real work carry the identity. Personality comes from existing evidence and
presentation, never invented anecdotes or claims.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, cobalt technology themes, glass cards, decorative
dashboards, stock imagery, and a wholesale B4 replacement. B4 contributes project
composition and career chapters; it does not replace the Wally identity.

## Design Principles

- Keep the approved hero composition and all existing copy, claims, imagery, and links.
- Let projects carry the strongest evidence; use chapters to connect the career story.
- Use open reading layouts and intentional contrast instead of unnecessary boxes.
- Make atmosphere subordinate to readable content and usable controls.
- Keep JD selection, ordering, and navigation scoped to each role.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA text contrast, visible keyboard focus, complete active cards, and
responsive layouts down to 320px. Reduced motion and no JavaScript must leave all
content readable. Decorative effects cannot intercept input or obscure text.

## Revision boundary

The approved September 8 visual-system pass is local to the revision worktree on
codex/wally-portfolio-revision. No publishing, original-checkout edits, canonical
case-study changes, or existing employer-package rebuilds are part of this pass.
