An unbranded campaign for National Night Shift Workers' Day. I managed it across the NASDAQ board in Times Square, national TV, Xbox Live, radio, outdoor and consumer journals, working through the media, digital and print vendors.

The direct-to-consumer launch on the agency's largest pharma business. I was one of two consumer account leads, managing a $20M brand budget.
