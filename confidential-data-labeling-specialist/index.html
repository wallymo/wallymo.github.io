03 / AI Implementation · 2023–now

Building with AI lets me take ideas from A to Z.

The work starts before the model: find the decision a client needs to make, narrow it to the smallest useful proof, then design evidence and human review into the product.

Prove one thing first
Each POC answers one business question, giving the client evidence for what is worth building next.
Build the whole loop
Connect intake, model behavior, interface, sources, and review so the demo works like a product.
Change the actual job
One pharma route fell from eight hours to ten minutes; MLR review prep went from three weeks to four days.
