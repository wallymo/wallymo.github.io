# Wally Mostafa - Regulated-Sector UX/HCD Project Summaries

## Regulated Pharma Workflow Platform | Hedgehox | 2024-Present

As UX and AI implementation lead, I translate pharma-agency needs and FDA, HIPAA, MLR, evidence, privacy, routing, and operational constraints into practical, human-reviewed product workflows. The work spans claims review, brief intake, scoping, submission capture, screenshot routing, and FDA-signal monitoring. For Claims Detector, I designed a workflow that identifies promotional claims, surfaces evidence gaps, and pre-populates audit-ready annotations while keeping people responsible for review decisions before MLR submission. I moved the concept through workflow definition, interface design, functional prototyping, demo-story development, and stakeholder iteration; the working POC helped secure an investor. The program demonstrates how I make high-trust automation explainable and usable without hiding uncertainty or removing human oversight.

## Patient and HCP Digital Experience Design | Heartbeat Ideas and Omnicom | 2018-2020

As a senior and freelance UX designer supporting pharma, healthcare, and oncology accounts, I led research and experience strategy across patient, healthcare-professional, and internal brand-team journeys. I translated stakeholder input, competitive audits, user needs, content requirements, and regulated review constraints into journey maps, site maps, user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and practical UX recommendations. I worked across strategy, account, creative, engineering, and delivery teams so findings and usability feedback became build-ready experiences that could move through medical, legal, and regulatory review. The work is direct evidence of regulated healthcare HCD methods, cross-functional facilitation, and constraint-aware digital-service design.
