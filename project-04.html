User Research

Kinesso (IPG) · Lead UX Designer & Researcher

I built the UX research discipline from scratch across a 20+ product Marketing Intelligence Engine, giving product, design, and development teams a repeatable way to learn from users.

The Setup

A research discipline had to be created before the products could become user-centered.

  • Challenge Research was recognized as important, but there was no structured, consistent discipline across the product suite.
  • Solution A strategic roadshow, reusable tools, Figma documentation, and a flexible process that could fit different product verticals.
  • Impact A stronger user-centric culture, clearer collaboration, better product decisions, and a path to dedicated research support.

Research foundation

The first job was making research operational.

Stepping in to create the UX discipline from scratch, I positioned user research as the cornerstone for reshaping a suite of 20+ marketing intelligence products.

  • Blank canvasResearch existed as an idea, but not as a practical system teams could run repeatedly.
  • Workflow gapWithout a standard process, research varied by team and often produced misaligned insights.
  • User trustA growing perception that research was a facade had to be addressed through genuine inclusion and follow-through.
Roadshow

The roadshow connected research to the Marketing Intelligence Engine.

I worked across Audiences, Activation, Optimization, and Reporting leads to show how research could improve decisions, user experience, business alignment, and continuous iteration.

  • Informed decisionsUser insights helped teams minimize assumptions and anchor changes in real-world needs.
  • Product alignmentResearch bridged user expectations and business objectives across product verticals.
  • IterationFeedback became part of an ongoing loop rather than a one-time checkbox.
Research System

Tools and documentation made the discipline repeatable.

  • TypeformCaptured real-time feedback and helped teams analyze results quickly.
  • UseberryTurned prototype testing into actionable insight from interactive user flows.
  • HeapMeasured product behavior and grounded design choices in live interactions.
  • Microsoft TeamsSupported interview recording, transcription, and a searchable research archive.
Process

The research process flexed, but the foundation held steady.

The operating model gave teams enough structure to move consistently while adapting to each product, audience, and timeline.

  • The listUser lists spanned super-users to newcomers, with regional diversity to surface local behavior and constraints.
  • SchedulingPMs scheduled interviews with advance context, clear goals, and focused 30-60 minute sessions.
  • FindingsStandardized reports made insights easier to compare, share, and turn into product decisions.
Figma Documentation

Research artifacts became reusable team infrastructure.

White-glove experience

Participants had to feel like collaborators, not test subjects.

The program relied on consistent communication, personalized follow-up, open-access knowledge, and dedicated beta groups for real-time feedback.

  • CommunicationRegular project-status updates kept participants feeling valued and in the loop.
  • Knowledge accessA Figma research library and workshops helped others champion research at their own pace.
  • Beta groupsDedicated groups helped product suites gather feedback and refine more quickly.
Research became a working system, not a one-off activity.
